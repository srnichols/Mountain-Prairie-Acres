    Key points

    How these maps were made

    • Contours (1 m interval), flow paths, and divides traced from the owner's USGS 1 m elevation maps (October 6, 2026), scaled to the fences: 332 ft fence to fence, 1,320 ft from the north line to the south line.
    • Elevation between contours interpolated on a 5 ft grid (smooth surface through the contour lines), sampled every 10 ft. 1 ft contours, slope, flow arrows, profiles, and the main divide come from that surface.
    • Driveway traced from the USGS aerial; shed and container from their roofs; the house pad is the high ground inside the 1451 m contour. Orchard layout from the pollination grid (October 4, 2026), with R1 about 15 ft inside the south end of the 600 ft strip.
    • Soil: USDA NRCS Web Soil Survey report for the property, map unit 149, Simonton loam, 2 to 12 percent slopes.
