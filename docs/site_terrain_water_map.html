    Key points

    How these maps were made

    • Elevations: USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP) 1 m lidar, fitted to the fence lines on USGS NAIP aerial imagery (331 × 1,292 ft fence to fence) and smoothed to about 5 ft. 1 m and 1 ft contours, slope, flow arrows, profiles, and spot heights come from that surface (data/site_terrain.js). The lidar was flown before the homestead pads were graded.
    • Flow paths, the main divide, the split line, and the drainage areas come from routing water downhill across that surface (each 5 ft cell to its steepest neighbor).
    • Homestead, driveway, and yard from the owner's drone photos (October 2026), fitted to the same imagery and lidar; planned buildings where the owner described them (data/site_grid.js). Orchard layout from the pollination grid (October 4, 2026), with R1 about 15 ft inside the south end of the 600 ft strip.
    • An earlier version of these maps traced the owner's USGS 1 m map screenshots; the screenshots were tilted, which shifted features by 10–30 ft, so the maps now use the lidar directly.
    • Soil: USDA NRCS Web Soil Survey report for the property, map unit 149, Simonton loam, 2 to 12 percent slopes.
