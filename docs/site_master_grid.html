Mountain Prairie Acres: Site Master Grid

One coordinate system for every project on the property: lot feet from the northwest fence corner, 50 ft cells A–G by 1–26, tied to latitude and longitude. Pages and code helpers all read the same data file.

Reading a grid reference

Lot feet: x is feet east of the west fence, y is feet south of the north fence. The origin (0, 0) is the northwest fence corner, and the fences run true north–south and east–west.

Cells: a column letter (A–G, west to east) plus a row number (1–26, north to south). Each cell is 50 × 50 ft, so B19 is x 50–100, y 900–950. Write a spot as B19 (x 63, y 921).

SiteRef: B19-E13-S21 means 13 ft east and 21 ft south of B19's northwest corner. See the SiteRef guide and examples on the second printed page.

Feature coordinates and SiteRefs

SiteRefs below identify feature centers, not the full extent of a building or water line. Generated codes round to 0.01 ft; this does not improve the map's roughly 10 ft GPS accuracy.

Row laterals and valve boxes

Eight 10 ft, 1.5 in laterals (water_lateral_row_1 to 8) run west off the 2 in main, each ending in a 1 in riser into a valve box (a row head station), numbered south to north on the map. Their spacing along the main is read from the owner's drone photo (October 2026): the five in the south are about 60 ft apart and the three in the north about 70 ft apart. The hydrants' valve boxes (4 ft back on the hydrant's line, where it has one) follow the head stations.

SiteRef Location Codes and Shared Helpers

Format: CELL-Eoffset-Soffset fits a precise location in one printed column. C17-E12-S18 means 12 ft east and 18 ft south of C17's northwest corner: x 112, y 818. Decimals are feet, not inches; C17-E12.5-S18 is valid.

Planted example: the M-111 rootstock at orchard R6-C3 is B11-E33-S0 (x 83, y 500) in the current layout. SiteRef locates the spot, not the plant: keep the metal tag and individual Plant ID separately. A replacement plant can reuse the spot's code.

Unknown locations: record PENDING, never guessed offsets. The three Urban apples' outside-orchard locations are pending. Keep cell and offsets as underlying values and generate SiteRef for printing; do not maintain a second editable copy.

GPS input: the shared metadata stores one latitude/longitude tie for the lot. Each cell's northwest-corner GPS coordinates are calculated from its x0, y0 values, not stored as a second table. Convert a GPS point with SiteGrid.fromLatLon(latitude, longitude), then generate its SiteRef. Latitude comes first; Idaho longitudes are negative. Near cell boundaries, phone GPS error plus the roughly 10 ft map accuracy can put a point in the neighboring cell; verify measured offsets.

Web pages load generated data/site_grid.js and data/site_terrain.js, then call SiteGrid. Edit canonical JSON in the repository's data/ directory; see the property data and SQLite guide.
