Workshop Pole Barn: Sections and Construction Details

Cross-sections through the building, the two framing plans, and seven layer-by-layer details · 32 × 48 ft · 14 ft eave · gambrel

The package: Overview · Specs · Plans and elevations · Sections and details · Trusses · Materials and cost

These are design-intent drawings for the engineer, the truss plant, and the builder. Truss members and connections are placeholders until the truss designer and the engineer size them. Layers in the details are exaggerated where they say so. The markers match the tables under each drawing.

What Is in This Section

  1. Section A, across the width, looking east: the gambrel truss with its collar tie, the loft floor, the camper in the door, and the pad's fill under the slab.
  2. Section B, along the length, looking north: the loft floor, the collar ties, the stairs, the main door, the hoist beam and one of its braces, and the deck.
  3. Plan F1, foundation and base framing: piers, posts, the splash board, and the slab joints, on the pad.
  4. Plan F2, loft floor framing: the 25 truss bottom chords at 2′ OC, the stair opening, the loft door header, the hoist beam and its braces, and the deck.
  5. Detail 1 wall assembly · 2 roof assembly · 3 pier and post base · 4 rainscreen base · 5 eave vent-out · 6 loft door, hoist beam, and deck · 7 truss bearing.
  6. Detail 5 in perspective: the wall and the eave cut through a post and its truss, with the rainscreen's air path from the base to the eave and the heel block that joins the two chords.

Detail 1: Wall Assembly

MarkerLayer
A26 ga steel siding, vertical panels, barn red, fastened to the furring with #10 × 1½″ hex screws with EPDM washers
B1×4 PT furring at 24″ OC, vertical: makes a ¾″ ventilated rainscreen cavity
CTyvek CommercialWrap, taped seams, shingle-lapped, fastened with cap nails
D½″ OSB sheathing over the girts, APA Exposure 1, 8d ring-shank nails at 6″ on the edges and 12″ in the field (4″ at corners and openings)
E3-ply 2×8 laminated PT post at 8′ OC, on a wet-set bracket (Detail 3)
F2×6 SPF #2 girt, laid flat on the post (1½″ thick, 5½″ tall), 24″ OC, continuous over the posts with its joints on post center lines, as the two boards meeting here do. Each board is screwed to the post (the dark pins): 2 structural screws at a board end, 3 where a board crosses a post

Detail 2: Roof Assembly

MarkerLayer
A26 ga steel roof panel, factory Dripstop on the underside, beige, cut to the full slope length; #10 × 1½″ hex screws with EPDM washers in the manufacturer's high-snow pattern
BSynthetic underlayment (Titanium UDL30 or equal) over the entire roof
CIce and water shield: FULL on the lower (steep) slope; at the pitch break and around penetrations on the upper slope
D⅝″ OSB roof deck with H-clips between the purlins
E2×6 SPF purlins on top of the truss chords, 16–24″ OC per the truss design
FPre-engineered gambrel attic truss, 32′ span, 2′ OC, PE stamped for 120 psf ground snow

I2×6 PT base girt, laid flat on the post at the floor line (13″ above the pad): the lowest girt row, which backs the lower edge of the sheathing

Detail 6: Loft Door, Hoist Beam, and Landing Deck

MarkerWhat it is

KSteel plate on the beam's tip, with an ear for each brace; it is also the trolley's outer stop. The hook hangs a few inches in from it
