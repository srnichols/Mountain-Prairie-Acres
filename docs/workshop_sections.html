Workshop Pole Barn: Sections and Construction Details

Cross-sections through the building, the two framing plans, and seven layer-by-layer details · 32 × 48 ft · 16 ft eave · gambrel

The package: Overview · Specs · Plans and elevations · Sections and details · Materials and cost

These are design-intent drawings for the engineer, the truss plant, and the builder. Truss members and connections are placeholders until the truss designer and the engineer size them. Layers in the details are exaggerated where they say so. The markers match the tables under each drawing.

What Is in This Section

  1. Section A, across the width, looking east: the gambrel truss, the loft floor, the camper in the door, and the pad's fill under the slab.
  2. Section B, along the length, looking north: the loft floor, the stairs, the main door, the hoist beam, and the deck.
  3. Plan F1, foundation and base framing: piers, posts, the splash board, and the slab joints, on the pad.
  4. Plan F2, loft floor framing: the 25 truss bottom chords at 2′ OC, the stair opening, the loft door header, the hoist beam, and the deck.
  5. Detail 1 wall assembly · 2 roof assembly · 3 pier and post base · 4 rainscreen base · 5 eave vent-out · 6 loft door, hoist beam, and deck · 7 truss bearing.

Detail 1: Wall Assembly

MarkerLayer
A26 ga steel siding, vertical panels, barn red, fastened to the furring with #10 × 1½″ hex screws with EPDM washers
B1×4 PT furring at 24″ OC, vertical: makes a ¾″ ventilated rainscreen cavity

Detail 2: Roof Assembly

MarkerLayer
A26 ga steel roof panel, factory Dripstop on the underside, beige, cut to the full slope length; #10 × 1½″ hex screws with EPDM washers in the manufacturer's high-snow pattern
BSynthetic underlayment (Titanium UDL30 or equal) over the entire roof
CIce and water shield: FULL on the lower (steep) slope; at the pitch break and around penetrations on the upper slope
D⅝″ OSB roof deck with H-clips between the purlins
E2×6 SPF purlins on top of the truss chords, 16–24″ OC per the truss design
FPre-engineered gambrel attic truss, 32′ span, 2′ OC, PE stamped for 120 psf ground snow

D2×10 PT splash board on the outside of the post
E5″ slab, #4 rebar at 16″ OC each way
F4″ rigid foam, 10 mil vapor barrier, and 4″ compacted gravel, on the compacted fill

HNative soil: the pier pad bears here, or on fill the engineer accepts

Detail 6: Loft Door, Hoist Beam, and Landing Deck

MarkerWhat it is
A5′ × 6′ insulated double doors at the loft floor, locking from inside, opening clear for the hoist
B(2) 2×10 LVL header above the loft doors (engineer-sized)
CW6×9 or W6×12 steel hoist beam, about 12 ft: about 9 ft inside and 3 ft out, 9′ above the loft floor, rated for 500–1,000 lb
DEPDM weather boot and flashing where the beam passes through the wall: a CRITICAL detail
EAnchor plate and bolt-through hardware to the truss top chord, with blocking between trusses to spread the load. The truss manufacturer reviews it
FDeck framing: doubled 2×10 PT beams, 2×10 PT joists at 12″ OC, decking on top
G6×6 PT posts on 18″ Ø piers, 60″ below grade, in post bases
H42″ railing (fall protection above 30″)
IDiagonal knee braces: a 16 ft tall deck will sway without them
