Mountain Prairie Acres: Photo Map

Where each drone photo was taken, drawn on the lot with north up. Pick a numbered marker or a thumbnail to see the photo; the shaded patch shows roughly the ground the camera was looking at. The photos show the homestead, the orchard rows going in, and the water-line trenches, from the owner's drone flights in the fall of 2026.

Circle: an angled shot. The pointer shows the way the camera faced. Square: a straight-down shot. The pointer shows the top of the photo. Shaded patch: roughly the ground in view.

All photos, oldest first

    Each marker is the drone's GPS position when the photo was taken, placed on the master grid; it is good to roughly 10 to 30 ft. Heights are above the point where the drone took off, not above the ground under it. Markers that would overlap are moved apart, with a dotted line back to the true spot. The photos here are web-sized copies of the owner's originals, which stay in the owner's files, and the copies carry no location or camera data. The blue dots and teal squares on the map are the hydrants and valve boxes, as on the other maps.
