Metal Tag to Orchard Position — Planting Checklist

Start at the south end of the orchard with R1 and work north. Within each row, start at C1 on the west/windbreak side and work east toward C13. Match the exact metal Tag ID first; the tree name and inventory row are cross-checks. When several trees share one Tag ID, the sequence identifies each otherwise identical inventory instance.

Spot SiteRef: CELL-Eoffset-Soffset locates the listed orchard spot. Both offsets are feet from the master cell's northwest corner; B10-E33-S35 is the M-111 at R6-C3. E and S are compass directions, not page directions on this south-up map. Codes stay with vacant spots for 2027 replacements; the Urban apples' new outside-orchard locations remain PENDING.     Full Tree Inventory by Row

    Living orchard trees and records of vacated planting positions, south (R1) to north and west (C1) to east. Dead and outside-orchard trees retain their original planned slots as history; those slots are open, not their current locations. They are excluded from orchard counts and pollen partners. The planted M-111 rootstock occupies R6-C3 but is not counted as a fruit tree or pollen partner without a recorded scion cultivar. The Pineapple's open slot moved from R8-C3 to R8-C1 when the owner found the Asian pear (PR-Asian) in R8-C3. Original workbook fertility values are retained.

    Spot SiteRef identifies the listed planned or vacated orchard position, not an outside-orchard tree's current location. It is generated from the shared grid coordinates and is separate from the metal tag and individual Plant ID.
