Mountain Prairie Acres

Orchard and ranch projects · Prairie, Idaho · Map and directions

Plans, field checklists, and the data behind them for our place in Prairie: a 10-acre lot on the high desert, with an orchard going in north of the homestead, a west windbreak, and water-harvesting earthworks to make the most of the snowmelt. Each project below links to its page and a printable PDF.

Cultivar Guide: Plant Profiles

Plain-language profiles for 137 cultivars, species, and rootstocks in the inventory, the purchase list, and your seed list: fruit, nut, and berry plants, windbreak and shade trees, and perennials.
